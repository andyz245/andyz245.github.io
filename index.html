I build AI systems that do science.

I'm a co-founder of Intology, a research lab automating the process of discovery. We build Artificial Scientists: systems that read the literature, form a hypothesis, run the experiments, and write up what they found — with no human in the loop.

Two exist so far. Zochi was the first AI system to pass peer review at a top-tier venue. Locus is the first to outperform human experts at AI R&D.

Before Intology I did research at Meta and Virtue AI, and founded Lapis Labs, a student-led group that published 20+ papers with collaborators including Google DeepMind, the Center for AI Safety, and IBM Research.
